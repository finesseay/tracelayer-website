## 04 / Models and tools receive context

When a task uses a model or connected tool, relevant context is sent to that provider. It can include instructions, conversation history, files, source code, browser content and tool results. Provider responses can return to TraceLayer and become part of the stored task record. A locally running browser or Companion does not mean its task content stays on the device.

Provider handling depends on the provider, account, product, region and settings. A personal subscription, commercial API and enterprise account may have different training and retention terms. Using your own credentials does not by itself prevent provider processing, retention or human review. Review the terms of the services you connect.

Before authorising a connection, check that your provider plan permits the integration and the intended processing, and that you have permission to share the task content. A provider you independently contract with may act as a separate controller for its own purposes. A provider we engage to process personal data on our behalf is subject to our applicable service-provider obligations. Connecting an account does not, by itself, determine either party’s legal role or transfer all privacy responsibility to you.

We will not use Customer Content, as defined in the [Terms](../index.html#your-content-and-outputs), to train shared or general-purpose models without a separate, informed opt-in. Improving operational reliability does not create a general permission to train on private work.

## 05 / The trace is a stored record

TraceLayer preserves task conversations, run records, tool activity, approvals and saved results so that work can be resumed and reviewed. These records can include personal data, source code, file contents and information returned by connected services. A record may preserve information that has since changed in its source system.

Redacting information from a displayed trace is not the same as deleting the underlying record. Deleting a conversation, Task or Space may leave repository history, immutable output snapshots, run evidence and backup copies. This distinction also applies when an agent’s temporary working environment is removed.

These records remain subject to the retention criteria and deletion process described below. Their technical design does not override privacy rights.

## 08 / Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as needed for its stated purpose and any applicable legal requirements. The criteria below guide how long each category is kept. We consider the purpose of the record, the duration of the service relationship, outstanding requests or disputes, and legal obligations.

| Record | Retention criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Account and support records | For the account relationship and resolution of support requests, with further retention only where needed for legal obligations or disputes |
| Workspace content, traces and saved outputs | For workspace history and continuity of your work, subject to deletion requests and any lawful need to preserve evidence |
| Connection credentials and sessions | While needed for a valid connection or session, subject to revocation or expiry |
| Security and operational logs | A bounded period justified by incident response and reliability needs |
| Website measurement | A bounded period appropriate to the measurement purpose and applicable choices |
| Invoices and legal records | The period required by the applicable law or a documented legal hold |
| Backups and snapshots | Until backup rotation, with restrictions on use and deletion requests reapplied after a restore |

Stopping a task, revoking an account connection and erasing previously stored information are separate operations. Revoking a connection stops future authorised access as revocation takes effect; it does not automatically remove information already included in a trace or delete copies held by an independent provider.

You may request deletion at [hello@tracelayer.com](mailto:hello@tracelayer.com). We will verify authority, assess the request under applicable law, explain any lawful exception and act within the applicable deadline. Where immediate backup deletion is not feasible, retained copies must be isolated from ordinary use and removed through backup rotation.

## 09 / Cookies and website measurement

Authentication cookies and similar storage support sign-in and session security. Local preferences may remember choices such as appearance. Non-essential analytics must be distinguished from these functions and handled under the applicable consent or exemption rules.

Website measurement uses PostHog hosted in the EU and covers page categories, pseudonymous session identifiers, page exits, foreground engagement and selected link clicks, along with bounded campaign tags and the referring host. It excludes form contents, full query strings, fragments and raw link targets. Autocapture and session replay are disabled in the website configuration. The configuration also supports Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and opt-out flags.

Where consent is required, non-essential storage and collection must wait for it, and you must be able to withdraw consent. You can also use your browser’s storage controls and supported privacy signals. Contact [hello@tracelayer.com](mailto:hello@tracelayer.com) for help with your privacy choices.

## 10 / Security

Use proportionate access and review consequential work. We will assess personal-data incidents and notify affected customers, individuals or authorities where required by law or contract.
